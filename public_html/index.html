One system, end to end

Your server does the heavy lifting.

Tater Tube Server scans your movies and shows, resolves artwork, remembers playback, builds your Tube TV schedule, chooses direct play or transcoding for each screen, and can optionally enhance compatible video with AI upscaling and HDR Enhancement.

It is a complete self-hosted media platform—not another skin over Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin.
